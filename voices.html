Voices on this computer

No Japanese voice yet?

  1. Open Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region and add Japanese.
  2. Keep Text-to-speech ticked in the language features, finish the install, then restart your browser.
  3. Edge and Chrome usually list more voices (including online “Natural” ones) than LibreWolf or Firefox.

System voices are not trained anime voices, so pitch and a good female voice get you close. A neural anime voice (like the GPT-SoVITS voice in Project Riko) is on the roadmap.
