# Vesper — Productivity, Privacy, and Presence

## Presentation script

### Scene 1 — The invitation
**On screen:** Vesper’s welcome screen opens with a soft atelier glow and the line “Make space for what matters.”

**Narration:** “Meet Vesper: a private, local-first productivity companion with a living 3D presence. She is designed to make everyday planning feel lighter, more expressive, and easier to return to.”

### Scene 2 — A three-step beginning
**On screen:** The onboarding wizard moves through Personal Notes, Project Planning, Study / Research, and Daily Routine.

**Narration:** “New users do not face an empty dashboard. Vesper asks what you are here to do, creates three starter templates for that purpose, and offers a short guided tour. Skip it at any time—the workspace remains yours.”

### Scene 3 — Capture without friction
**On screen:** Press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Space. The Quick Capture window appears over the desktop. A text note, a link, and a voice note are added.

**Narration:** “When an idea arrives, capture it immediately. Quick Capture accepts text, web clippings, image links, files, and speech notes without opening the full studio. Every item is saved locally first.”

### Scene 4 — Shape the day
**On screen:** Items move across the Kanban board, then appear on the calendar. The dashboard widgets are rearranged.

**Narration:** “The Workspace turns raw captures into a gentle system: a draggable dashboard, a status board, a responsive calendar, checklists, recent items, and a time tracker. Moving a card updates its status automatically.”

### Scene 5 — A small rhythm
**On screen:** The activity heat map fills gradually. A seven-day streak badge appears.

**Narration:** “Vesper tracks lightweight habits locally—check-ins, notes, and completed tasks. The activity heat map and milestone badges encourage continuity without noisy notifications or public comparison.”

### Scene 6 — Privacy by design
**On screen:** The Offline Ready badge changes to Offline Mode. The Security & Sync panel shows a local PIN, auto-lock, biometric option, and a disabled cloud endpoint.

**Narration:** “Offline is not an error state. It is a supported mode. LocalStorage and IndexedDB keep CRUD operations available without a connection, while a sync queue waits quietly for a trusted endpoint. A local PIN, auto-lock, Lock Now hotkey, and optional WebAuthn protect the visible workspace.”

### Scene 7 — The companion layer
**On screen:** The 3D VRM avatar waves, blushes, listens, and speaks. The transparent Model Only window moves beside another application.

**Narration:** “The companion layer gives the workspace warmth. Vesper’s anime-inspired styling borrows the playful, flamboyant energy associated with Astolfo—bright reactions, teasing confidence, and expressive movement—without cloning a performer’s voice or redistributing copyrighted character assets. Installed offline voices drive speech, facial timing, lip sync, and blush reactions.”

### Scene 8 — Make it yours
**On screen:** Warm Sunset changes to Cyberpunk, then Forest Green. A custom accent and background are selected. The read-only publish dialog creates an expiring link.

**Narration:** “Choose the atmosphere, accent, font, and background that help you focus. Export to Markdown, CSV, PNG, or PDF. When collaboration is useful, publish a read-only view with optional password protection and expiry.”

### Closing — The promise
**On screen:** Vesper’s dashboard, offline badge, activity heat map, and avatar share the frame.

**Narration:** “Vesper is a private place to think, plan, remember, and begin again. It works locally first, syncs only when you choose, and keeps a little personality in the room.”

## Presenter notes

- Say **Astolfo-inspired energy** rather than claiming official affiliation.
- Describe English and Japanese system voices as **anime-dub delivery profiles**, not a cloned actor or character performance.
- Demonstrate offline mode by disconnecting the network, adding a card, refreshing, and reconnecting.
- Demonstrate the transparent overlay on more than one monitor by moving the Model Only window and restarting it to show saved position recovery.
- For cloud sync, explain that Vesper ships with an optional authenticated endpoint adapter; teams should provide an HTTPS sync service with their own identity, encryption, retention, and recovery policies.
