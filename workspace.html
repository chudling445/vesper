APP LOCK

Your PIN or master password protects the visible Vesper interface on this device. It does not upload or delete your local data. This is an interface lock, not encryption at rest; someone with access to this browser profile can still inspect locally stored files. Vesper stores the authenticator public key, not your fingerprint or face data; keep your PIN as the recovery method.
