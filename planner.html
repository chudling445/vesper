Fund feeds are checked hourly, but NAV publication is normally once per business day and may be delayed on holidays or by the provider. Not an offer or advice
Enter principal, published/user-entered annual rate, and tenure to see the estimate.

Formula: monthly EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n−1), where monthly r = annual nominal rate ÷ 12 and n = months. This assumes a fixed monthly reducing-balance rate. Taxes, insurance, floating-rate changes, lender rounding, legal/valuation charges, and other fees are excluded unless entered.

INDIA / GLOBAL / HISTORICAL REFERENCE

Observed market stress, not crash prediction

Higher normalized readings mean more stress in the selected sourced indicators. A lower reading means those indicators eased; it does not establish why or predict what happens next.

Loading stress source status…
RECORD A NORMALIZED INDICATOR

Keep the raw value and scoring method visible

2-hour feed checks

Checks run every two hours only for configured authorized feeds. Scores are supplied with their source-specific normalization method; they are not calibrated crash probabilities. Aggregate arithmetic: average indicators within each represented category, then give each represented category equal weight (1 divided by the number of represented categories). Missing categories are excluded, never scored as zero.

The 2008-style panel is a dated historical comparison only; no equivalence to current conditions is inferred. Credit, liquidity, breadth, volatility, currency, and flow data can be delayed, incomparable, or unavailable. No crash timing, probability, certainty, intervention causality, or financial advice is provided.

India market feeds are opt-in. No retailer, exchange, AMFI, fund-house, or lender site is scraped by default. Manual records and imports must retain source and timestamp details. Historic coverage depends on data you provide; no past prices, returns, NAVs, or lending terms are invented.
